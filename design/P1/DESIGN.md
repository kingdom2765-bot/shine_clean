## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmosphere of understated architectural luxury, absolute clinical hygiene, and technical precision. Designed for executive decision-makers, clinic directors, and high-end healthcare spaces (dermatology clinics, surgical centers, boutique wellness institutions), it avoids both the pedestrian clichés of domestic housekeeping (bubbles, sparkles, bright yellow sponges) and the cold, distressing sterile atmosphere of an emergency hospital.

The visual narrative relies on **Minimalist Clinical Editorialism**:
- Expansive, luminous negative space that implies immaculate air quality and surfaces.
- Strict architectural discipline through proportional grids and restrained micro-borders.
- Imagery directed strictly toward museum-grade architectural healthcare interiors, structural surfaces (natural stone, acoustic plaster, seamless linoleum), and macro documentation of certified sterilization procedures.
- Quiet authority conveyed through deep ocean-navy typography paired with calming mineral sage accents.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to invoke sterile balance, quiet confidence, and tactile organic cleanliness.

- **Primary (`#182A38` - Deep Navy)**: Replaces harsh pure black. Used for display typography, authoritative headings, and high-emphasis focal points. Evokes institutional longevity and meticulous precision.
- **Secondary (`#8CAAA0` - Muted Sage)**: A mineral green derived from clinical botanical solutions and filtered surgical lighting. Used for non-disruptive accents, verified validation markers, state badges, and measured progress indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#3D5667` - Slate Navy)**: Middle-range tone designated for supporting captions, metadata, technical specifications, and secondary actions.
- **Neutral Surface / Line (`#E5EBE9` - Sterilized Ash)**: Structural hair-width dividers, container borders, and quiet resting surfaces.
- **Base Backgrounds**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) forms the pristine core canvas, layered with an air-washed Off-White (`#F8FAF9`) for structural grouping and rhythm.

## Typography

Typography establishes architectural balance and high-density legibility. Plus Jakarta Sans mirrors the disciplined geometric structure and humanist apertures of contemporary Korean typographic frameworks (Pretendard and SUIT), ensuring universal cross-platform rendering while maintaining severe editorial precision.

- **Headlines**: Tight letter-spacing (`-0.03em` to `-0.015em`) with deliberate line-height control eliminates visual clutter, emphasizing stature and quiet competence.
- **Body**: Generous leading (`1.6` to `1.7`) guarantees effortless scanning of protocol descriptions and air sanitization metrics.
- **Labels & System Meta**: Tracked widely (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) and rendered in uppercase or title-case, mirroring pharmaceutical serialization codes and ISO cleanroom certification markings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an architectural **12-column grid** on desktop (max width 1320px) transitioning to a **4-column grid** on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: Outer canvas margin of `4rem`, column gutters of `1.5rem`. Macro vertical sections must breathe with generous spatial cadence (`5rem` to `8rem` padding), mirroring the airy openness of clinical atrium architecture.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout, gutters compress to `1.25rem`, margins adjust to `2.5rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Strict 4-column flow with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen margins. Heavy multi-column spec comparisons reflow into vertical sequential blocks or horizontal friction-free touch carousels.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial separation is achieved through **tonal planes and micro-borders** rather than heavy drop shadows.

- **Tonal Tiers**:
  - `Base`: Canvas Background Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - `Tier 1`: Subtle Enclosure Off-White (`#F8FAF9`) framed with a 1px border of `#E5EBE9`.
  - `Tier 2`: Elevated Modals or Tooltips in Pure White with a 1px border of `#DDE5E2`.
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines**: Divisions rely on 1px solid borders using `#E5EBE9`. No blurred drop shadows are permitted on static informational cards.
- **Floating Overlays**: For sticky navigation headers and diagnostic dropdowns, apply an architectural backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) combined with 90% opacity `#FFFFFF` and an ultra-diffused ambient shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(24, 42, 56, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces **Level 1 (Soft)** rounding (`0.25rem` / 4px base radius) to reflect precise engineering and architectural discipline. High-radius or pill-shaped geometries undermine the clinical gravitas and must be strictly avoided.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Cards)**: `4px` corner radius.
- **Grouped Structural Panels**: `8px` corner radius (`rounded-lg`).
- **Data Badges & Protocols**: `2px` micro-radius or crisp zero-radius corners to maintain clinical instrumentation aesthetics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Background Deep Navy (`#182A38`), text Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), `height: 48px`, horizontal padding `24px`, radius `4px`. Hover shifts to `#233B4E` with an ease-out 150ms transition.
- **Secondary CTA**: Background transparent, border 1px solid `#8CAAA0`, text `#182A38`. Hover state fills with an ultra-light sage tint `rgba(140, 170, 160, 0.08)`.
- **Ghost/Text**: Text Deep Navy (`#182A38`) with an understated underline offset or accompanied by a micro-chevron.

### Cards & Service Units
- Built on `#F8FAF9` or `#FFFFFF` with a crisp 1px `#E5EBE9` border.
- Generous internal padding (`space-lg` / `1.75rem`).
- Never use elevated box-shadows on hover; instead, transition border color cleanly from `#E5EBE9` to `#8CAAA0` with an imperceptible 1px Y-axis shift.

### Badges & Technical Indicators
- Background `rgba(140, 170, 160, 0.12)`, text `#3D5667`, 1px solid `rgba(140, 170, 160, 0.3)`.
- Typographic style: `label-tech` (`11px`, tracked `0.08em`).
- Used to signify ISO Cleanroom classifications, ATP bioluminescence readouts, and air exchange ratings.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E5EBE9`, text `#182A38`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#8CAAA0` with an external 2px ring at `rgba(140, 170, 160, 0.2)`. No harsh primary outlines.
- Labels utilize `label-md` seated cleanly above the field, maintaining high spatial rhythm.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- Checkboxes: 18px square, 2px radius, 1px solid `#8CAAA0`. Checked state renders Deep Navy (`#182A38`) with an off-white geometric checkmark.
- Segmented Protocol Switchers: Pure White pill/tab resting inside an Off-White (`#F8FAF9`) inset track with a 1px `#E5EBE9` perimeter.

### Diagnostic Comparison Module (Domain-Specific)
- A clinical metrics table juxtaposing standard commercial cleaning against technical surgical sanitization. Uses vertical hairline separators, clean typographic numerical alignment, and muted sage tick-metrics for certified standards.
