## Brand & Style

This design system expresses medical sanitation and environmental hygiene not through sterile cliches—such as bright turquoise droplets, harsh hospital greens, or hyper-clinical chemical graphics—but through the quiet authority of architectural preservation, high-end hospitality, and spatial stewardship. 

The brand narrative positions environmental sanitation as an invisible luxury: the absolute purity of air, surface, and light within premier healthcare institutions. The emotional response is one of composure, profound safety, and timeless architectural grace.

The visual style unites **Minimalism** with an **Architectural Editorial** framework:
- **Restrained Monoliths:** Layouts borrow from architectural monographs and high-end spatial journals, prioritizing expansive negative space, deliberate cadence, and fine hairline structural dividers over gratuitous decoration.
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Moving decisively away from cold, antiseptic clinical blues, the interface embraces soft natural mineral tones, evoking tactile stone, unbleached linen, and filtered natural light.
- **Typographic Precision:** Typographic tension balances razor-sharp, low-contrast structural tracking with deliberate scale jumps to convey clinical authority without clinical anxiety.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in architectural earth and stone, projecting clinical integrity through natural composure:

- **Primary (`#292F2D` - Dark Charcoal):** Used for authoritative typography, primary actions, high-contrast borders, and strong grounding focal elements. It provides deep editorial legibility without the harshness of pure black.
- **Secondary (`#625A50` - Warm Secondary):** Used for sub-captions, supporting editorial prose, technical specifications, and mid-tier interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#9C8974` - Subtle Warm Ochre / `#B3A28E` - Muted Sand):** Serves as an architectural point accent. Applied selectively to validation indicators, status indicators of purity, micro-badges, and refined visual markers.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F3` - Ivory):** The primary canvas background. It softens viewport illumination, emulating matte rag paper.
- **Supporting Tiers:**
  - `Surface Container Lowest` (`#FFFFFF`): Pure white reserved for focal cards, elevated modals, and precision data plates.
  - `Surface Container` (`#EFEAE2` - Warm Neutral): Applied to secondary structural panels, table headers, and sunken structural wells.
  - `Border / Hairline` (`#E5DFD5`): Soft stone divider line used strictly at 1px to organize information architectural grids.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes editorial balance and architectural clarity over tech-centric density. `Plus Jakarta Sans` is selected for its refined geometric clarity, with fallback pairing to system-native `Pretendard` for East Asian regional contexts.

- **Editorial Headlines (`display-lg`, `headline-xl`):** Set at regular weights (`400`) with tight letter-spacing to emulate high-end architectural journals. Large headlines should never be bolded aggressively; presence is established via scale and generous negative space rather than heavy strokes.
- **Editorial Body Flow (`body-xl`, `body-md`):** Generous line height (1.6x–1.66x) allows eyes to scan detailed hygiene standards and technical spatial protocols without fatigue.
- **Structural Meta Labels (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase with open letter-spacing (`0.12em`). Used exclusively for protocol numbers, room zoning taxonomies, ISO air-quality indices, and category anchors.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model employs an asymmetrical, editorial fluid grid balanced across 12 architectural columns on desktop, 8 columns on tablet, and 4 columns on mobile.

- **Asymmetry & Editorial Cadence:** Key landing views alternate wide data monoliths (8 columns) with contextual margins or meta-summaries (4 columns), avoiding rigid dashboard symmetry. Empty space is treated as an active spatial element, mirroring unencumbered physical space.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, `margin: 4rem`, `gutter: 2rem`. Content max-width is pinned to 1440px to preserve editorial balance.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column grid, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Multi-column editorial modules collapse gracefully into single-column vertical scrolls with fine horizontal rules between sections.
- **Structural Rhythm:** Section transitions rely on `space-xl` (72px) margins to preserve calm pacing, ensuring environmental hygiene metrics are never compressed or claustrophobic.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic drop shadows, floating bubbles, and aggressive blur filters. Visual depth is established strictly through **Tonal Layering** and **Architectural Hairlines**:

- **Tonal Stepping:** 
  - Ground Floor: Ivory (`#FAF8F3`) base canvas.
  - Recessed/Secondary Tier: Warm Neutral (`#EFEAE2`) for specification sidebars, metadata strips, and inactive panels.
  - Elevated Focal Tier: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for focal data blocks, active verification cards, and inspection modal sheets.
- **Low-Contrast Structural Outlines:**
  - Elevated surfaces do not cast heavy ambient shadows. Instead, boundaries are articulated by a crisp 1px solid border (`#E5DFD5`).
- **Subtle Ambient Ambient Accent (Modals / Overlays):**
  - When overlays demand physical elevation, use an ultra-diffused, warm-tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -12px rgba(41, 47, 45, 0.06), 0 0 0 1px #E5DFD5`.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy is strictly **Sharp (`0`)**.

By employing 0px border radii across all interactive containers, data modules, buttons, and input fields, the interface references architectural floor plans, stone slabs, and physical print publications. 

- Sharp, rectilinear corners convey precision, discipline, and uncompromising engineering.
- Visual comfort is achieved through warm color palettes and generous internal padding (`space-md`), never through softened or rounded geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Dark Charcoal (`#292F2D`) background, Ivory (`#FAF8F3`) text, 0px radius. Hover shifts to Subtle Warm Ochre (`#9C8974`). Padding: `space-sm` vertical, `space-md` horizontal. Label styled with `label-md`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with a 1px Dark Charcoal (`#292F2D`) border and Dark Charcoal text. Hover fills with Warm Neutral (`#EFEAE2`).
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** Dark Charcoal text with a permanent 1px underline in Subtle Warm Ochre (`#9C8974`), positioned 4px below baseline.

### Chips & Badges
- Rectilinear tags with 0px corner radius.
- Background in Warm Neutral (`#EFEAE2`) with a 1px border in `#E5DFD5`. Typography set to `label-caps` in `#625A50`.
- Active or Purity status chips: Ivory (`#FAF8F3`) background, Dark Charcoal (`#292F2D`) border, preceded by a 4px solid Ochre (`#9C8974`) square glyph.

### Cards & Modules
- Primary canvas cards: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) background bounded by a 1px `#E5DFD5` border.
- Layout: Asymmetrical internal distribution. Generous padding (`space-lg`). Content organized using hairline dividers (`#E5DFD5`) rather than nested cards.

### Input Fields
- Understated architectural inputs: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid border `#E5DFD5`. Text in Dark Charcoal (`#292F2D`).
- Focus state: Border transitions to 1px Dark Charcoal (`#292F2D`), with no glow or halo.
- Labels: Placed above the field using `label-caps` in `#625A50`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes: 16px square, 0px radius, 1px border `#625A50`. Selected state fills with `#292F2D` with an Ivory square indicator or hairline check.
- Radio buttons: 16px square (retaining sharp architectural geometry), outer border `#625A50`, selected state features a solid 8px `#292F2D` central square.

### Lists & Data Tables
- Continuous horizontal hairline rules (`1px solid #E5DFD5`). Vertical borders are omitted.
- Row padding: `space-md` vertically to create a readable, unhurried tempo.
- Table headers: Uppercase `label-caps` in `#625A50`, left-aligned with strict typographic alignment to cell data.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Environmental Metric Plate:** Minimalist architectural stat display featuring a massive numeral (`headline-xl`), a hairline divider, and ISO sanitation or microclimate air quality classifications styled in `label-caps`.
- **Zoning Protocol Accordion:** Monolithic ivory/charcoal collapsibles displaying surgical suite clearance standards, clean-room airflow diagrams, and surface hygiene verification logs.
